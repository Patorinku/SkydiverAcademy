# Skydiver Academy Landing Page

## Overview
Build a premium, long-form landing page that positions Skydiver Academy as an industry training and partnership organization for game art, 3D, animation, and game development.

## What will be built
- Cinematic first screen with strong game-production artwork, clear positioning, and workshop/partnership actions
- Credibility section centered on Patrick Benai’s 10+ years of production experience
- Six visual training-discipline cards with production-focused descriptions
- Dedicated pathways for students, institutions, and game studios
- Four-step engagement process and a prominent featured workshop
- Replaceable portfolio gallery for characters, weapons, hard surface, props, environments, real-time work, and cinematics
- Academy differentiators, founder biography, partnership pathways, contact form, and concise footer
- Responsive navigation and layouts intentionally adapted for desktop, tablet, and mobile

## Visual direction
- Deep charcoal and graphite surfaces with a restrained burnished-copper accent
- Bold condensed display typography paired with a highly readable modern sans-serif
- Cinematic 3D production imagery, tight crops, fine borders, and generous negative space
- Minimal, production-studio UI rather than academic or online-course styling
- Subtle motion, image depth, and refined interaction states with reduced-motion support

## Technical details
- Reusable React data-driven sections and shared interaction patterns
- Semantic design tokens in the global theme; no page-level hardcoded colors
- Generated original artwork stored locally for fast loading and easy future replacement
- Accessible labels, keyboard-friendly navigation, responsive images, and contact controls
- Complete page metadata for search and social sharing

## Scope note
The contact form will provide a polished front-end submission experience. Persistent delivery requires a connected email or data service and is not included in this page-only build.
