# Skydiver Academy

Create a premium, modern landing page for Skydiver Academy, an industry-led training academy focused on video game art, 3D, animation and game development.

Brand positioning

Skydiver Academy aims to help develop the next generation of African digital artists and game developers by connecting professional industry experience with practical, production-focused education.

The academy is founded by Patrick Benai, a Senior 3D Character and Weapons Artist with more than 10 years of professional experience in video games and cinematic production.

Skydiver Academy should feel like a combination of:

A premium AAA game studio

A modern technology company

A high-end digital art academy

An industry training organization

Do NOT make the website look like a traditional university website. It should feel creative, cinematic, technological, ambitious and international.

The primary audiences are:

Aspiring game artists and developers

Universities and technical schools

Professional game studios

Training institutions and organizations

The website should communicate that Skydiver Academy provides industry-oriented training, workshops and customized professional programs, rather than simply offering conventional academic courses.

VISUAL DIRECTION

The visual identity should be premium, cinematic and technological.

Think:

AAA game studio

Unreal Engine presentation

Modern creative technology company

Premium digital art portfolio

Minimalist academy

Avoid:

Generic education templates

Corporate blue university websites

Excessive academic imagery

Stock photos of students sitting at computers

Cheap gaming aesthetics

Excessive neon colors

Cartoonish graphics

Use:

Large professional 3D artwork

Dark sophisticated backgrounds

Strong typography

Large headlines

Generous negative space

Subtle gradients

Fine borders

Minimal UI elements

Cinematic image crops

Subtle animations

Smooth scrolling

Elegant hover effects

The website should feel expensive, professional and international.

Use a restrained color palette based primarily around dark neutral tones with one sophisticated accent color.

Typography should be modern, bold and highly readable.

RESPONSIVE DESIGN

Make the entire website fully responsive.

Desktop:

Wide cinematic layouts

Large artwork

Multi-column sections

Tablet:

Simplified grids

Maintain visual hierarchy

Mobile:

Strong typography

Large but optimized imagery

Easy-to-use CTA buttons

Simplified navigation

Cards stacked vertically

Fast loading

The mobile version should feel intentionally designed rather than simply being a compressed desktop layout.

IMPORTANT DESIGN PRINCIPLES

The website should communicate three things within the first 10 seconds:

1. This is about the video game and digital entertainment industry.

2. This is led by someone with real professional production experience.

3. Skydiver Academy works with students, institutions AND professional studios.

The primary conversion goal is:

REQUEST A WORKSHOP

The secondary conversion goal is:

START A PARTNERSHIP

Do not make the site feel like an online course marketplace. It should initially feel like an industry training and partnership organization.

Build the page with reusable components so that additional programs, workshops, instructors, student projects and case studies can easily be added later.
